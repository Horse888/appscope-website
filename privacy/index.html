Scope

This policy applies to AppScope Developer Dashboard for iOS, iPadOS and macOS, provided by ZuYuan Zhou / Sixplus. AppScope is an independent developer tool and is not affiliated with Apple or Google.

Accounts and authorized reports

When you connect your own App Store Connect account, AppScope uses the API key and account identifiers you provide to request your apps, sales reports and customer reviews directly from Apple. When you authorize Google AdMob, AppScope uses Google's OAuth service and tokens to request your account information and advertising reports directly from Google. When you authorize read-only Google Play financial reports, the app downloads sales and earnings ZIP/CSV files from the private Cloud Storage report bucket you configure, parses them on the current device, and caches the parsed records locally. Sales records may include order identifiers, dates, financial status, app and product identifiers, amounts, currencies and buyer country. Earnings records include transaction dates and types, app identifiers, merchant currencies and amounts. These reports are not bank payout records. The app does not automatically read Google Payments payout history. These requests identify the connected account. Report data is displayed and cached on your device; AppScope does not operate a separate server to aggregate your sales or advertising revenue.

Google permissions and Limited Use

Google sign-in requests your account identifier and email to label and distinguish your connections. AdMob read permissions support publisher and app discovery and advertising reports. Google Play reports require the Cloud Storage read-only permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/devstorage.read_only). Google's consent screen describes access to Cloud Storage data because the permission is broader than one Play report bucket. When you choose to discover report buckets and provide a Cloud project, AppScope asks Google for bucket names beginning with pubsite_prod_rev_ in that project; the project must grant your Google account permission to list buckets. You choose a result or enter a bucket manually. The report reader then requests only the configured Play financial report bucket and its sales and earnings report paths; it does not provide a general Cloud Storage browser or write, delete or permission-management functions. Google's existing account and bucket access controls still apply. AppScope's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide the account and reporting features you select. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to train AI models or sent to AI providers. AppScope does not upload Google OAuth tokens or financial reports to a developer-operated server. Google connection metadata may sync through your own iCloud account as described below; Google authorization tokens and Play report caches stay on the device. The developer does not routinely receive or review your reports. If you contact support, provide only the information you choose to share and avoid financial records or credentials.

Local storage and iCloud

Account credentials and authorization tokens are stored in the system Keychain. Account directories and display preferences can synchronize through your iCloud account. Apple API private keys marked as synchronizable can synchronize through iCloud Keychain. Each device separately authorizes Google access. Reports, customer reviews, response drafts and authorized operational snapshots can be cached on the device. Apple processes iCloud and Keychain services under its own terms and privacy policy.

Replies and translation in this version

AI reply features are paused in this version. It provides no AI-key setup or AI draft generation and sends no reviews, feedback or reports to DeepSeek, Doubao or other AI providers to generate replies. You can edit drafts manually. The app submits a review reply to Apple or an administrator feedback reply to the associated CloudKit service only when you separately confirm submission. On supported systems, Apple's Translation framework translates reviews and feedback on demand. Language models may need an initial download. Translations are reading aids; original text is retained and translations do not automatically become public replies. Support depends on the system, device and language pair.

Purchases and membership

Apple's App Store services process payments, trials, subscription renewals, cancellation and refunds. AppScope reads StoreKit-verified transactions and entitlements to unlock membership features and provides a restore-purchases control. It does not upload purchase receipts to a separate developer-operated server. The app does not receive your card number or payment-account credentials. Apple's terms and privacy policy govern its transaction records and related processing.

Operational analysis

The operational panel is intended for authorized administrators of the associated SuperClock CloudKit container. It can display existing product-usage records, pseudonymous account or installation identifiers, usage profiles and feedback from that service, and send administrator responses or read receipts. Access depends on CloudKit server permissions. This is separate from tracking the AppScope user's use of other companies' apps. SuperClock's own policy governs collection by SuperClock.

Exchange rates and network services

Exchange-rate services receive the required dates and currencies, not your account keys, financial amounts or customer reviews. Apple, Google and exchange-rate services may process connection metadata such as IP addresses according to their own policies. The public product and policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website connection metadata under its own privacy policy. These information pages do not initiate Google authorization or request your account credentials.

Tracking and advertising

AppScope does not embed an advertising SDK, access IDFA, sell personal data, or combine data from other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising or advertising measurement. Displaying AdMob reports does not mean that AppScope serves advertisements. AppScope does not add a separate product-usage analytics upload.

Retention and choices

Local caches and drafts remain on your device until removed by the app's supported controls, device storage management or app deletion. Removing a Google connection from this device removes its stored OAuth state and requests removal of the associated local AdMob and Play report caches; no source report is deleted from Google. On macOS, removing the application bundle alone may leave its app data. Keychain and iCloud items can remain until separately removed. Disconnect an account in Settings to stop using it; revoke Google access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections or revoke an Apple API key in App Store Connect to prevent further authorized requests. Disconnecting in AppScope and revoking at Google are separate actions. These steps do not delete source-platform reports, published replies or third-party provider records. Data retained by Apple, Google and iCloud follows their own retention and deletion controls. To request assistance with access, correction or deletion of data held by Sixplus, contact tecentmoon.feedback@gmail.com. Please never send API keys, private keys or authorization tokens.

Children and updates

This tool is intended for developers and is not directed to children. We will publish policy changes with an effective date.
